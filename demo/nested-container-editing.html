Nested-container move/resize regression demo

outside sits directly in the Model (parent origin 0,0). inside_a / inside_b sit inside box, a Container placed well away from the canvas origin — the exact shape of the bug in stickyflow.js#9 (an entity's absolute canvas position was written straight into its parent-relative layout.position).

Drag or resize inside_a / inside_b a little. They should stay roughly where you dropped them, inside box, and the SFL below should show a small delta on their existing position — not a huge jump. Drag box itself too: its children should ride along and stay correctly placed inside it.
